# 13 — Marketing

## Constraints (read first)

- Owner is full-time employed at a bank → no public face, no LinkedIn, no podcasts/conferences
- Async-only — no sales calls, demos, customer interviews
- Pure self-serve product
- Anonymous/pseudonymous brand identity OK

These constraints **eliminate ~70% of typical SaaS marketing tactics** but the remaining 30% is enough.

## Positioning frames

### 🥇 Frame 1: "Strategy above tools" (lead with this)
> *"Klaviyo sends emails. We tell you which emails to send."*

Powerful because:
- Not positioned as Klaviyo competitor (low-friction sale)
- Leverages existing investment in execution tools
- Solves real pain (have tools, lack strategy)
- Easy to grasp in 5 seconds

### 🥈 Frame 2: "Banking-grade for your store"
> *"The customer lifecycle frameworks that protect $300B in bank deposits, now applied to your Shopify store."*

Authority transfer + sophistication signal.

### 🥉 Frame 3: "Stop guessing, start orchestrating"
> *"You're sending emails. That's not a lifecycle program."*

Pain-point framing for paid ads.

## Acquisition tactics (ranked by ROI for our constraints)

### 🎯 Tactic 1: The "Free CLM Audit" tool — #1 acquisition hook

A standalone landing page that does mini-product-demo before signup:

1. Visitor lands at `[domain].com/audit`
2. Connects Stripe (read-only, no signup needed)
3. System runs analysis
4. Visitor sees: **"Your CLM Maturity Score: 47/100"**
5. Free output: stage distribution, top 3 critical gaps, revenue left on table
6. Email gate to unlock: full strategy report (12-page PDF)
7. CTA: "Implement these — try LifecycleAI free"

Why it works:
- ✅ Zero customer interaction required
- ✅ Demonstrates value BEFORE signup (massive trust)
- ✅ Generates qualified leads (connected Stripe = real merchant)
- ✅ Shareable ("I got 47/100 — time to fix this")
- ✅ Educates the market on CLM as a category
- ✅ Low cost (just LLM + storage)

**This single tactic could drive most acquisition.**

## Marketing thesis

Most e-com marketing tools market on tactics ("our automations are better"). We market on **outcomes + sophistication** ("banking-grade lifecycle, applied to your store"). The educational angle is the moat:

- Klaviyo can't write "The Banker's Guide to E-com Retention"
- The owner's background is the credibility nobody else has
- Each educational piece compounds (SEO + authority + leads + product demo)
